How an engagement runs

A short path from a messy workflow to something the team will actually run.

  1. Frame

    Map the workflow, the data, and the decision. Agree what “working” means in numbers before anyone writes a line of code.

  2. Prove

    A thin slice under production conditions. Evaluation, failure cases, and a go or no go — not a slide deck of happy paths.

  3. Build

    Harden, integrate, and document. Hand over with the people who will own it, in the tools they already open on Monday.

  4. Embed

    Review cadence, incident paths, and the next use case. The system has to survive a quarter, not a demo day.
